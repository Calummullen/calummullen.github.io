Privacy policy

Heistoku · Last updated 17 October 2026

The short version

I don't collect any of your data. Heistoku has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing is ever sent to me. Your progress stays on your phone, with a backup copy in your own iCloud or Google account that Apple or Google look after.

Who I am

Heistoku is made by me, Calum Mullen, trading as Mullen95 Games, in the United Kingdom. You can reach me at mullen95games@gmail.com.

What the game stores

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your phone. It holds the cases you've solved, your stars and best times, your daily case results, your badges and your settings. It doesn't contain your name, email, location, contacts or anything else about you.

Backups

  • iPhone: a copy of that save is kept in your own iCloud using Apple's iCloud key-value storage, so it comes back if you reinstall the game or get a new iPhone. It's stored by Apple in your account. I can't see it or access it.
  • Android: the save can be included in your phone's own Google backup, if you have backups switched on. That's handled by Google in your account. I can't see it or access it.

Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover how they store your backups.

Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone you can use Apple's Game Center if you want to. It's optional, and the game works the same without it. If you're signed in to Game Center, your time for the daily case goes on Heistoku's daily leaderboard, and the badges you earn show up as Game Center achievements. Other players see them under your Game Center nickname.

Game Center is run by Apple, in your Apple account. I can't see your scores or anything else about you through it, and nothing comes to me. Apple's Game Center privacy page explains what Apple does with it, and you can turn Game Center off in your iPhone's Settings.

What I don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups.
  • No adverts, and no advertising or tracking IDs.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or other third-party tools that collect data.
  • Nothing is sent to me, and I don't store, sell or share anything.

If you email me

I'll use your email address and whatever you send only to reply to you. I'll delete it when I'm done, or straight away if you ask.

Children

Heistoku is suitable for all ages. Because the game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, it doesn't collect any from children either.

Deleting your data

In the game, go to Settings and choose Reset all progress. That clears the save on your phone, the copy in your iCloud, and your Heistoku achievements in Game Center. Daily leaderboard times can't be removed by the game, but each day's leaderboard starts fresh the next day. Deleting the game removes the save from your phone too.

Changes

If anything changes, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
