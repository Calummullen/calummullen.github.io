Heistoku

Find the mole

A logic puzzle. Read everyone's clue, place the crew on the floor plan, and work out who was alone with the Guard.

Help

How do I play?

Tap a crew member to read their clue, then tap a square to place them. Each row and each column holds exactly one person. The Guard was alone in a room with one other person, and that person is the mole. There's a short tutorial in the game, and How to play explains every type of clue.

I'm stuck

Tap the bulb for a hint. The first tap shows you where to look; tap again to see why. Every case can be solved by thinking it through, no guessing needed.

I think a case is wrong

Every case is checked to make sure it has exactly one answer. If you think you've found one that doesn't, email me a screenshot and I'll take a look.

Will I lose my progress if I get a new phone?

On iPhone, your progress is copied to your own iCloud and comes back when you reinstall the game while signed in to the same Apple account. On Android, it's included in your phone's Google backup if backups are switched on.

Do you collect my data?

No. I don't collect or store anything. Your progress stays on your phone and in your own iCloud or Google backup. Privacy policy.

How do I start again?

Settings, then Reset all progress. Tap it twice to confirm.
